The fitted golfer surfaces in assets/characters/X-golfer.zip and Y-golfer.zip are adapted from the CC0 MakeHuman hm08 base mesh. The player uses the existing Mixamo animation rigs.

https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj

cut-grass.webp is a resized and color-adjusted version of the Three.js terrain example's grasslight-big.jpg.

https://threejs.org/examples/textures/terrain/grasslight-big.jpg

The tour golfers use the CC0 MakeHuman system assets. Skin textures, modeled eyes, brows, short hair and bob, casualsuit06 and shoes05 were fitted to the existing player rigs. The fabric and hair maps are adapted for the creator's color controls.

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
