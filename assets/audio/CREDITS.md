## Golf impact recordings

- **drive.wav** uses “Single Golf Drive, Gold Coast, 2026” by cryany (RyanAudioCatalogue), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original recording](https://freesound.org/people/cryany/sounds/867451/)
- **putt-a.wav** and **putt-b.wav** use two strokes from “putt.wav” by 170129, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original recording](https://freesound.org/people/170129/sounds/408260/)

The game uses trimmed, high-pass filtered, mono excerpts with adjusted levels and fades. Club and swing strength affect playback.
